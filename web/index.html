What is xlog?

xlog helps you lock a rough cut faster. Drop in footage and it indexes scenes, dialogue, action, and emotion — then hands you a stringout of usable beats, the selects that match your brief, and alt takes beside the hero. Source timecode stays put. Nothing is deleted. Keep, dump, or open the handles, then send it to Premiere. It will not finish the film for you.
